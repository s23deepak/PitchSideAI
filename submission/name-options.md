# Naming Options

## Recommended: PitchSideAI

Why it works:

- Signals football/match context immediately.
- Matches the existing GitHub/repo identity.
- Suggests sideline intelligence, live analysis, and broadcast support without sounding generic.
- Works for fans, commentators, and broadcast teams.

Tagline:

Real-time football intelligence for fans and broadcast teams.

## Other Strong Options

### FanLens AI

Best if the product is primarily fan-facing. It matches the existing Fan Lens mode, but it is slightly narrower for commentator and production workflows.

### MatchDesk AI

Best if the product is positioned as a production/research desk for creators and commentators. More professional, less consumer-friendly.

### Sideline IQ

Memorable and brandable, but less explicit about vision and match footage.

### Broadcast Lens

Very clear for media teams, but less distinctive as a startup/product name.

### MatchSignal

Good for live event detection and tactical signals, though it sounds more like infrastructure than an end-user product.
